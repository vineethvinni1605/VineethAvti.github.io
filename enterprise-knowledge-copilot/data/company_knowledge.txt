NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES — ENTERPRISE KNOWLEDGE BASE

========================================
REMOTE WORK POLICY
========================================

Nexora Technologies follows a hybrid work model.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams determine their in-office collaboration days based on project requirements.

Employees working remotely must use company-approved devices and connect to internal systems through the corporate VPN.

Employees should remain available during core collaboration hours from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time.

========================================
PAID TIME OFF POLICY
========================================

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year.

PTO requests of three consecutive business days or more should be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

Managers are responsible for approving PTO requests based on project coverage and team availability.

Unused PTO may carry over into the following year up to a maximum of five days.

========================================
INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
========================================

Employees must use multi-factor authentication when accessing company systems.

Passwords must not be shared with coworkers or stored in unsecured documents.

Confidential company information must only be stored in approved enterprise systems.

Suspected phishing emails should be reported to the Security Operations team.

Company data must not be uploaded to unapproved third-party AI tools or external storage services.

========================================
EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
========================================

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business expenses.

Expense reports should be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date.

Receipts are required for individual expenses greater than $25.

Business travel must receive manager approval before booking.

========================================
AI USAGE POLICY
========================================

Employees may use company-approved AI tools for research, summarization, software development and productivity tasks.

Confidential customer information, employee personal information and proprietary company data must not be entered into public AI systems.

AI-generated content must be reviewed by an employee before being used for business decisions or external communication.

Employees remain responsible for the accuracy and appropriateness of AI-assisted work.

========================================
TECHNICAL SUPPORT
========================================

Employees experiencing account access problems should contact the IT Service Desk.

Security incidents, suspicious account activity and suspected phishing attempts should be reported to the Security Operations team.

Critical production incidents should be escalated through the engineering incident-response process.

Standard technical-support requests should include the employee's department, device type, issue description and relevant error messages.
